About the Workshop
Econometrics plays an important role in business and management research. It helps
researchers analyze data, test theories, and make informed decisions. This two-day
workshop aims to provide participants with both theoretical understanding and
practical exposure to econometric tools used in business research. The workshop will
focus on basic to advanced econometric techniques and their application using real
business data.
Objectives of the Workshop
To provide clear understanding of econometric concepts
To introduce commonly used econometric models in business research
To provide hands-on training in data analysis and interpretation
To enhance research skills of faculty members, scholars, and students
