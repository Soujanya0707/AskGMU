ABOUT GANGADHAR MEHER UNIVERSITY
Gangadhar Meher University, located in Sambalpur,
Odisha, is one of the leading state universities known
for its commitment to academic excellence, research,
and innovation. The university offers a wide range of
undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs
across disciplines and promotes interdisciplinary
research and intellectual engagement. The university
aims to nurture academic leadership and contribute
to national development through quality education
and research.
SCHOOL OF COMMERCE
The School of Commerce, G.M. University, Sambalpur, (erstwhile G.M. College, Sambalpur
established in the year 1944) is one of the oldest and largest among the Commerce Departments in
Odisha. The B. Com course was first time introduced in the State by this Department. The
Department offers courses like UG, PG Programme, and Ph.D. programme in Commerce.
THEME OF THE SEMINAR
Financial Technology (FinTech) has revolutionized the financial services sector by improving
accessibility, efficiency, and transparency. With the rapid expansion of digital payment systems,
mobile banking, blockchain technologies, and artificial intelligence, financial services are
becoming more inclusive and accessible. However, challenges such as digital literacy, regulatory
issues, cyber security, and rural accessibility remain significant barriers to achieving inclusive
growth. This seminar aims to bring together academicians, researchers, policymakers,
practitioners, and students to discuss the transformative role of FinTech in promoting financial
inclusion and sustainable economic development.
OBJECTIVES OF THE SEMINAR
To explore emerging trends in financial technology.
To examine the role of FinTech in promoting financial inclusion.
To analyze regulatory and cybersecurity challenges in digital finance.
To encourage interdisciplinary research in financial innovation.
To provide a platform for academicians, researchers, and practitioners to exchange ideas.
