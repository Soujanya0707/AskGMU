The School of Computer Science was established in the year of 1998 and offering the BSc Computer Science course under the aegis of the School of Physics. Later in the year 2001, MSc in Computer Science was introduced. It got its own identity as a fully independent department in the year 2017. The school started the PhD programme in the year 2020 after the recruitment of three faculty members along with supportive staffs. The curriculum emphasizes practical and theoretical aspects to prepare students for both industry and academia. The School has a library with good number of books to enrich students' understanding across various Computer Science disciplines. 

Currently, three PhD scholars are pursuing their doctorate, with one successfully awarded in the year 2023. Many students from the school are regularly qualifying for competitive exams such as NIMCET, CUET, NET, and GATE, and are pursuing further education at prestigious institutes.Several alumnus have secured placements in renowned organizations in India such as TCS, Accenture,WIPRO, DRDO, Deloitte, Kantar etc and also abroad. The school frequently hosts seminars and mentorship programs to guide students. It also promotes collaborative research through a conducive and disciplined academic environment and orient the students towards serving the society.

Vision to create globally competent undergraduates and postgraduates in Computer Science by imparting training in emerging technologies and collaborative research through a conducive and disciplined academic environment, and orient them towards serving the society.

Mission To provide high quality professional training at the undergraduate and postgraduate level with an emphasis on basic principles of computer science. To empower the students with the required skills to solve the complex technological problems of modern society and also provide them with a framework for promoting collaborative and multidisciplinary research. To strengthen the Industry-Academia interface by interacting with the industry, educational & research organizations and alumni that will help the students to emerge as leaders in academics or in entrepreneurship. To impart moral and ethical values, and interpersonal skills to the students for betterment of the society.

Alumni of the computer science department have been emerging as influential figures across a wide array of industries, leveraging their technical expertise to drive innovation and solve complex problems. Our graduates have become key players in the tech sector, assuming roles as software developers, data scientists, cybersecurity experts, and IT consultants. Many also channel their skills into entrepreneurship, founding startups that introduce groundbreaking technologies and services. Beyond the tech industry, computer science alumni are increasingly sought after in finance, education, government organizations and entertainment, where they apply their knowledge to optimize systems, enhance user experiences, and create new opportunities. Their diverse career paths and contributions underscore the transformative impact of computer science education on both industry and society.
